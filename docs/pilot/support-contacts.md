## Escalation Matrix

### Severity Levels

| Severity          | Definition                                 | Example                                          | Response | Resolution  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----------- |
| **P1 — Critical** | Platform down or data breach risk          | All users can't login, FERPA violation suspected | 30 min   | 4 hours     |
| **P2 — High**     | Major feature broken for multiple users    | IEP progress can't be saved, reports fail        | 1 hour   | 8 hours     |
| **P3 — Medium**   | Feature degraded but workaround exists     | Dashboard slow, notification delay               | 4 hours  | 24 hours    |
| **P4 — Low**      | Minor issue, cosmetic, enhancement request | Typo in UI, feature suggestion                   | 24 hours | Next sprint |

### Escalation Path

```
Level 1: Customer Success (support@aivo.com)
   ↓ (if not resolved in SLA)
Level 2: Engineering On-Call (PagerDuty rotation)
   ↓ (if P1/P2 not resolved in 2 hours)
Level 3: Engineering Lead + Product Manager
   ↓ (if data breach or compliance issue)
Level 4: CEO + Legal
```
