# AIVO Platform — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> **Audience**: All pilot district users (district admins, teachers, parents)  
> **Last updated**: Sprint 8

---

## General

### What is AIVO?

AIVO is a special education platform that helps schools manage Individualized Education Programs (IEPs), track student progress, and keep parents informed. It includes AI-powered tools to support personalized learning and streamline compliance workflows.

### Who is this pilot for?

This pilot involves Anoka-Hennepin ISD 11 with 4 pilot schools, approximately 50 teachers, 400 parents, and 500 students with active IEPs.

### How long is the pilot?

The initial pilot runs for 30 days. Based on results, we will expand to additional schools and districts.

### Is AIVO free during the pilot?

Yes. There is no cost to the district, teachers, or parents during the pilot period.

## Accounts & Access

### How do I log in?

- **Teachers**: Go to https://teacher.aivo.example and use your school email
- **Parents**: Go to https://parent.aivo.example and use the email from your welcome message
- **District Admins**: Go to https://admin.aivo.example

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page. You'll receive a reset link via email within 2 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

### My account is locked. How do I unlock it?

After 5 failed login attempts, accounts are locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact support at support@aivo.com.

### Can I use AIVO on my phone?

Yes. AIVO has mobile apps for both iPhone and Android:

- **iPhone**: Download via TestFlight (link in your welcome email)
- **Android**: Download via Play Store beta (link in your welcome email)

You can also use any mobile browser to access the web version.

### Can multiple family members access the same student's data?

Yes. Each parent/guardian receives their own account. Both can view the same student's IEP and progress data.

## For Teachers

### How do I see my caseload?

Your caseload appears on your dashboard immediately after login. It shows all students where you are the assigned case manager.

### How often should I log progress?

We recommend logging progress data at least **once per week** per goal. This ensures compliance with IEP progress reporting requirements and gives parents accurate, timely information.

### Can I collaborate with other teachers on a student's IEP?

Yes. Each student has a **Team** tab where you can see all educators involved. You can share notes and tag team members for notifications.

### What does the AI Tutor do?

The AI Tutor provides personalized, scaffolded instruction to students based on their IEP goals and functioning level. It adapts in real time and logs all interactions for teacher review. It supplements — but does not replace — teacher instruction.

### Can I turn off the AI Tutor for a specific student?

Yes. Navigate to **AI Tutor → Settings** and toggle the setting per student.

### How do I generate a compliance report?

Go to **Reports → Compliance**, select the scope (district/school/student), choose PDF or CSV, and click **Generate**. Reports typically generate within 5 seconds.

## For Parents

### What can I see on AIVO?

As a parent, you can view:

- Your child's current IEP goals and accommodations
- Progress data recorded by teachers (updated as it's entered)
- Upcoming IEP meeting dates
- Messages from the education team

### Can I edit my child's IEP?

No. IEPs can only be modified by the education team through the formal IEP process. You can view all current information and raise questions with your child's teacher through the messaging feature.

### How often is progress updated?

Teachers are encouraged to log progress weekly. You'll see new data as soon as it's entered by the teacher.

### Will I get notifications?

Yes. You'll receive email notifications when:

- New progress data is recorded for your child
- An IEP meeting is scheduled
- A message is sent to you by the education team

You can manage notification preferences in **Settings → Notifications**.

### Can I download my child's IEP?

Yes. Navigate to your child's IEP page and click **Download PDF**. You can also request a full data export by contacting support.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my child's data safe?

Yes. AIVO is designed with security and FERPA compliance as top priorities:

- All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Data is stored in the United States only
- Access is restricted by role (teachers see only their caseload; parents see only their child)
- Every data access event is logged in an immutable audit trail

### What is FERPA?

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) is a federal law that protects the privacy of student education records. AIVO fully complies with FERPA requirements.

### Who can see my child's data?

Only authorized members of your child's education team and you (the parent/guardian). District administrators can view aggregate data but individual access is logged.

### Can I request my child's data be deleted?

Student education records are retained for 7 years per FERPA requirements. After that period, records are securely deleted. For questions about data retention, contact your district administrator or support@aivo.com.

### Is AIVO SOC 2 certified?

SOC 2 Type II certification is in progress and expected for Phase 2. Currently, AIVO follows SOC 2 security controls including encryption, access control, audit logging, and incident response.

## Technical Issues

### The page won't load. What should I do?

1. Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
4. Check your internet connection
5. If the problem persists, contact support@aivo.com

### The mobile app crashes when I open it.

1. Make sure you have the latest version installed
2. Restart your phone
3. Uninstall and reinstall the app
4. If the problem continues, email support@aivo.com with your phone model and OS version

### Data looks incorrect or outdated.

1. Refresh the page to get the latest data
2. Check the "Last Updated" timestamp on the data
3. If data seems wrong, contact your child's teacher or support@aivo.com

### I'm getting an "Access Denied" error.

This means your account doesn't have permission to view that page. Possible causes:

- You're trying to access another student's data (only your child's data is visible)
- Your session expired — log out and log back in
- Your account hasn't been fully set up — contact support@aivo.com
