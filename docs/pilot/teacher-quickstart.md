# AIVO Platform — Teacher Quickstart Guide

> **Audience**: Special education teachers and case managers  
> **Time to read**: 8 minutes  
> **Prerequisites**: Teacher account credentials (check your email)

---

## Welcome, Teacher!

AIVO helps you manage IEPs, track student progress, and get AI-powered support for lesson planning and accommodation strategies — all in one place. This guide will have you up and running in under 10 minutes.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### First Login

1. Check your email for the AIVO welcome message
2. Click the login link or navigate to: **https://teacher.aivo.example**
3. Enter your school email and temporary password
4. Set a new password (minimum 12 characters)
5. You'll land on your **Teacher Dashboard**

### Mobile App (Optional)

- **iOS**: Download from TestFlight (link in your welcome email)
- **Android**: Download from Play Store beta (link in your welcome email)
- Log in with the same credentials as the web app

## 2. Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows everything relevant to your caseload at a glance:

| Widget               | What It Shows                                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **My Caseload**      | Students you're case manager for, with IEP status       |
| **Upcoming Reviews** | IEP annual reviews due in the next 30 days              |
| **Recent Progress**  | Latest progress data entries (yours and team's)         |
| **Action Items**     | Missing data, overdue entries, and compliance alerts    |
| **Quick Actions**    | Buttons for common tasks (log progress, view IEP, etc.) |

## 3. Managing IEPs

### Viewing an IEP

1. Click a student's name from your caseload
2. Select the **IEP** tab
3. You'll see the full IEP including:
   - Present levels of performance
   - Annual goals and short-term objectives
   - Accommodations and modifications
   - Related services schedule
   - Progress monitoring data

### IEP Goals

Each goal shows:

- **Goal description** with measurable criteria
- **Baseline** — where the student started
- **Target** — where they should be by review date
- **Current progress** — visual chart showing trend
- **Status** — On Track / At Risk / Not Met

### Updating Progress

1. From the student's IEP, click **Log Progress** on a goal
2. Enter:
   - **Date** of observation/assessment
   - **Score/measurement** (numeric or percentage)
   - **Notes** (observations, strategies used, etc.)
3. Click **Save** — the progress chart updates immediately

> **💡 Tip**: Log progress weekly to maintain compliance and give parents accurate data.

---

## 4. Accommodations

### Viewing Accommodations

Each student's IEP lists their required accommodations:

- **Presentation** (e.g., text-to-speech, large print)
- **Response** (e.g., verbal responses, extended time)
- **Setting** (e.g., small group, preferential seating)
- **Timing** (e.g., extended time, frequent breaks)

### Digital Accommodations

AIVO automatically applies certain accommodations in digital learning:

- Font size adjustments
- Text-to-speech activation
- Extended time on assessments
- Simplified interface mode

## 5. AI Tutor (NEW!)

The AI Tutor helps students learn at their own pace with personalized support.

### How It Works

- The AI Tutor adapts to each student's IEP goals and functioning level
- It provides scaffolded instruction in math, reading, writing, and science
- All interactions are logged and available for teacher review

### Teacher Controls

- Navigate to **AI Tutor → Settings** for your caseload
- You can:
  - Enable/disable AI Tutor per student
  - Set subject focus areas
  - Review conversation logs
  - See AI-generated progress insights

> **⚠️ Note**: The AI Tutor supplements your instruction — it doesn't replace it. Review AI sessions weekly to ensure alignment with IEP goals.

## 6. Compliance Dashboard

Navigate to **Compliance** to see:

- ✅ IEPs up to date
- ⚠️ IEPs with pending action items
- ❌ IEPs past compliance deadlines

### What Triggers Alerts

| Alert                        | Cause                                | Action Needed        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Annual review due            | < 30 days until annual review date   | Schedule IEP meeting |
| Missing progress data        | Goal has no data entries in 30+ days | Log current progress |
| Accommodation not documented | Service listed but not recorded      | Document delivery    |
| Parent signature needed      | IEP modified but not co-signed       | Contact parent       |

---

## 7. Reports

### Student Progress Report

1. Go to **Reports → Student Progress**
2. Select a student and date range
3. Click **Generate** → PDF download

### Caseload Summary

1. Go to **Reports → Caseload Summary**
2. View aggregate data for all your students
3. Export as CSV for your records

---

## 8. Collaboration

### Team Communication

- Each student has a **Team** tab showing all educators involved
- Use the **Notes** section to share observations with the team
- Tagged team members receive email notifications

### Parent Communication

- Parents can view (but not edit) their child's IEP and progress
- You can send updates to parents via the **Messages** tab
- All parent communications are logged in the audit trail

---

## 9. Tips for Success

1. **Log progress weekly** — Consistent data helps identify trends and maintains compliance
2. **Check your dashboard daily** — Action items appear when things need attention
3. **Use Quick Actions** — The dashboard buttons save clicks for common tasks
4. **Review AI Tutor logs** — See how students interact with AI-supported learning
5. **Ask for help** — The support team is standing by during the pilot
