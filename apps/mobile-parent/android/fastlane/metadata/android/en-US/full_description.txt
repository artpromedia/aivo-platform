AIVO Parent is the companion app for parents and guardians of children using AIVO Learn. Stay connected to your child's educational journey with real-time progress reports, achievement notifications, and detailed analytics — all from your phone.

KEY FEATURES:

• Real-Time Progress Tracking — See what your child is learning, how they're performing, and where they need support
• Achievement Notifications — Get notified when your child earns badges, hits streaks, or completes milestones
• Detailed Analytics — Visual charts and reports showing progress over time across all subjects
• Multiple Children — Monitor all your children's learning from a single dashboard
• Session History — Review completed lessons, scores, and time spent learning
• Secure Communication — Direct messaging with teachers and educators through the platform
• Privacy Controls — Manage your child's account settings and data privacy preferences

DESIGNED FOR PARENTS:
AIVO Parent gives you the insights you need to support your child's learning without being intrusive. Set learning goals, review progress, and celebrate achievements together.

STAYING CONNECTED:
Push notifications keep you informed about important milestones and daily activity summaries, so you always know how your child's learning is going.

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
Your family's data is protected with enterprise-grade security. We are fully COPPA-compliant and never sell or share your children's data.
